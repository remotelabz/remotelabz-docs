# Booking

## Basic concepts
Since RemoteLabz 2.4.4, it is possible for everyone to schedule physical laboratory reservations through a functionality called `Booking`.

![Screenshot](/images/booking/physical_lab_booking.png)

## Calendar
Each physical lab has his own reservation schedule .
Once on the booking page, click on the lab's page to view its schedule. This schedule is accessible to everyone, including students.

![Screenshot](/images/booking/Booking_calendar.png)

## Booking a lab
Everyone, including students can book a laboratory. To do this, click on New Booking.

![Screenshot](/images/booking/Booking_creation.png)

A new form appear. You need to fill in the following information :

- **Name of the practical work** : it justifies the booking (example : POO S1). 
- **Owner of the reservation** : greyed out for students, only a teacher or an administrator can assign a reservation to another user or group .
- **Start and end time** : booking can be scheduled on two years ahead max.
